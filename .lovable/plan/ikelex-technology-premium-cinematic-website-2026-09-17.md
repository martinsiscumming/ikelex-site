# Ikelex Technology premium cinematic website

## Foundation
- Enable Lovable Cloud for secure authentication, data, and image storage.
- Build a unified Ikelex design system from the supplied logo: deep navy, bright blue, cyan, white, and restrained red accents; strong editorial typography; consistent sharp radii, borders, shadows, and motion.
- Add the supplied wordmark as the primary brand asset and derive the browser icon from the supplied mark.
- Establish reusable page framing: adaptive floating navigation, animated mobile menu, footer, scroll progress, section reveals, magnetic actions, and floating contact menu.

## Public experience
- Build distinct route-based pages for Home, About, Services, Contact, and seven individual service pages.
- Create the Home page as a cinematic scroll narrative: immersive animated technology core, scrubbed statement, pinned business-needs story, asymmetric service field, large software showcase, QR-menu transformation, editorial principles, animated process, staggered gallery, database-backed reviews, service marquee, and final contact invitation.
- Give About, Services, Contact, and each service page their own composition while preserving one visual language and animation hierarchy.
- Use original rendered interface visuals and lightweight animated geometry rather than fake company imagery, statistics, logos, awards, or testimonials.
- Keep motion scroll-linked where appropriate, reduce intensity on mobile, lazy-load heavy visuals, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Contact experience
- Show the supplied contact person, phone, WhatsApp number, and Port Harcourt address without inventing a website or email.
- Validate the contact form in the browser and on the server, save inquiries securely, then offer a prefilled WhatsApp continuation.
- Read email and other editable details only from administrator settings; hide unavailable contact methods gracefully.

## Secure administration
- Provide a private `/admin` login and dashboard for one administrator, with no registration or social login.
- Support username-based sign-in through a protected administrator profile and role record, with credentials validated server-side.
- Add administration screens for inquiries, reviews, page imagery, and contact/social settings.
- Allow review creation, editing, deletion, publishing, unpublishing, rating, service/date/photo fields, and explicit verified-customer status.
- Allow previewing and replacing all requested page images through managed storage.

## Data and safety
- Store administrator profiles and roles separately, with strict access rules and server-side role verification.
- Store settings, inquiries, image assignments, and reviews with explicit grants and row-level access controls.
- Public pages may read only published reviews and public settings; unpublished content and all mutations remain administrator-only.
- Render a polished empty review state until genuine published reviews exist.

## Quality checks
- Add unique metadata for every public route without a domain or fabricated email.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, forms, admin protection, review visibility, WhatsApp continuation, reduced motion, scroll interactions, and image loading.
- Check the live preview for visual overlap, animation smoothness, runtime errors, and build errors before completion.
